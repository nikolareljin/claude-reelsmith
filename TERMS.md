## Content you generate

Titles, captions and descriptions may be written by an AI model reading sampled
frames and transcripts. Models misread names, mishear announcements and invent
plausible details.

**Check the output before publishing it.** The tool renders a plan and one
sample clip and asks for approval specifically so this check has a place to
happen. A misattributed performance is a real harm to a real person, and it is
trivially avoidable.

Musical works may be in copyright even when a performance is yours.
