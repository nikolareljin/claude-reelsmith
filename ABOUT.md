## Why this exists

reelsmith started as a single-purpose script for one piano recital: a folder of
phone clips, a hand-written programme, and a lot of manual ffmpeg. The audio
tuning in it was hard-won and genuinely good. Everything around it assumed one
event, one resolution, and a human willing to type every title.

This is that tuning, generalised — and handed the one thing a script cannot do,
which is look at a clip and understand what it contains.
